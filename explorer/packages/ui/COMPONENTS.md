# UI library component reference

Per-component notes for `packages/ui/src/domain/`: props, layout, and the rules a component keeps. The rules for writing components (levels, folder shape, copy catalogue) are in `explorer/CLAUDE.md`.

## Theme: surfaces, shadows and the panel (`theme/`)

Depth comes from the theme, so a component states no fill, border or shadow of its own for these.

- **Surfaces.** `bg` is the ground (and inset wells), `panel` the card fill, `raised` (`#1A212A`, the value of `bgDot`) lifts controls, `overlay` (`#1E2630`, the value of `map.none`) is for what floats. They are separate names because a surface is a different use from a dot or a map class. Tokens are in `theme/tokens.json`; `palette.graf.raised` and `palette.graf.overlay` carry the colours.
- **Shadows.** `theme.graf.shadow` (`shadow` from `theme/tokens.ts`) has `panel`, `raised`, `overlay`, `well` and `header`. Used by: panel on every Card, raised on outlined buttons, overlay on menus, popovers and tooltips, header on the sticky header; `well` is for inset areas and is applied through the `Well` atom (see "The main screen's panels"), not by the theme. `theme.graf.logoShadow` is the logo disc's. `theme.graf.headerFill` is the translucent `bg` the header and footer share.
- **Panel.** Any MUI `Card` (so `Panel` (and `SignalCard`, which renders one), `GoToCard`, `ValueFacts` and the hand-rolled panels that now render a `Card`: the map region, the Coverage note and data-through cards, the Signal rules cards, the entity headline figures and framework line) gets the panel fill, a 1 px `line` border, the card radius and the panel shadow from the `MuiCard` override. A new panel is a `Card` with layout `sx` only (padding, gap); never `bgcolor`, `border` or `boxShadow`. `GoToCard` alone overrides the border colour (accent, to mark the pinned card). Not panels, and untouched: `StateNote`, the framed `CiteLine`, row dividers.
- **Overlays.** `MuiTooltip`, `MuiMenu` and `MuiPopover` use the overlay fill, a `lineStrong` border, the card radius and the overlay shadow. The `Menu` and `MenuItem` atoms (#224) wrap MUI's and get this from the theme; no Popover wrapper exists yet. `MapTooltip` (the map's own tooltip) uses the same tokens.
- **Buttons.** Outlined buttons are raised (`raised` fill and shadow; a disabled one keeps the fill, no shadow). Hover, pressed, selected and focus are unchanged.
- **Header and footer (`app/src/components/AppShell`).** From `md` up the header is sticky, `headerFill` with a 10 px backdrop blur, the header shadow, and a z-index above menus (above the modal layer); below `md` it scrolls away, since its nav wraps. Because it covers the top of the viewport, the theme sets `scroll-padding-top` (`layout.headerScrollPadding`, 120 px) on `html` from `md` up, so in-page anchors land below it; anchored targets need no margin of their own. The logo disc has its shadow; the wordmark is unchanged. The footer takes the same fill and blur.
- **Amber** is reserved (see `explorer/CLAUDE.md`, "Amber"). The Theme story `Shadows` shows each shadow.

## UI vocabulary (the other-pages handoff, `docs/handoffs/grivei-explorer-other-pages`)

The words used for the parts of a page, so a ticket, a story and a review mean the same thing. They live here, not in `GLOSSARY.md` (domain terms only).

- **Panel.** A titled `Card`: header strip, body, optional foot strip (`molecules/Panel`, next section).
- **Header strip.** The band at a panel's top: the strip fill (`rgba(13,16,20,.35)`, `FilterStrip`'s `STRIP_FILL`), a `line` hairline below, 9 px 16 px padding, at least 28 px of content. Left, the mono 13 accent heading; right, an optional meta line or one control.
- **Foot strip.** The band at a panel's bottom: the same fill, a hairline above, 10 px 16 px, mono 12 muted. A count, a caveat, paging or a "show all" link.
- **Well.** A sunk surface (`atoms/Well`: ground fill, `line` border, inset shadow). It holds a map, an inset figure, an input or a segmented control's track (`SegmentedTrack`).
- **Inset figure.** A figure in a well (`molecules/InsetFigure`): label, value, and an optional note line. Used where a number supports a headline.
- **Segmented control.** A row of mutually exclusive options on a well track, the chosen one in the selected state (`OptionToggle`, `SourcePicker` and `SignalOrderToggle`: the map's lens, the money lens's side, the Signal order, the Relationships "Show" toggle, the Source picker; see "The segmented control"). Not a tab strip and not a menu.
- **Tag.** A neutral, outlined label for an attribute of what it sits on: a person's role, a Record's channel, "SEAP counterparty" on an ONRC row. It carries no judgement and no count, and is never amber. There is no `Tag` component yet: `ChannelTag` is the one tag, and a second use is the moment to lift a shared `Tag` atom from it (an outlined `Chip` with the `ChannelTag` look: 1 px `lineStrong` border, `radius.chip`).
- **Badge.** Only a Record badge (`BadgeChip`: `GLOSSARY.md`, "Record badge"). **A Record badge is never called a tag, and a tag is never called a badge.** Code that says "badge" for anything else (`AnafStatusBadge`) predates this rule and keeps its name until it is touched.

Decision on the labels the handoff calls badges. None of these is a Record badge, so none uses `BadgeChip`; and each is a statement about the thing it sits on, so each is a tag in the sense above, with the tag look, not an ad hoc chip:

| Label | Tag? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ONRC status ("active", "radiat") | Yes | An attribute of the registration, shown as published. The date it holds since is text beside it, not part of the tag. |
| "current" (the ONRC name in use) | Yes | An attribute of one name among several. |
| "in force" (a Leadership row's period is open) | Yes | An attribute of the row. Its dates stay as text. |
| A statement's revision ("revised") | Yes | An attribute of one statement year; the first-release and revised-by lines stay as text under the source lines. |
| "SEAP counterparty", a person's role, a Record's channel | Yes | As above. |

Their form today differs: ONRC statuses are filled `Chip`s (`OnrcResultRow`), "current" is a text prefix (`AliasList`), "in force" is text inside the period string (`LeadershipSection`), a statement's revision is text (`FinancialYearRow`), and only "SEAP counterparty" and `ChannelTag` are outlined. They keep that until the shared `Tag` atom is lifted; the tickets that rework each panel move them to it, and none takes amber or a Record badge's tooltip.

## The titled panel (`domain/molecules/Panel`, #244)

`Panel` is the one titled box. Props: `heading` (a string, or a node for header content beyond text such as the Signal card's ring beside its rule label: then `label`, the region's accessible name, is required), `label` (the region's name when it differs from the heading), `level` (`h3` by default; `h2` on a page whose title is the `h1`), `id` (the anchor), `aside` (the header strip's right slot: a meta line, mono 12 muted, or one control), `foot` (the foot strip; absent means no strip), `flush` (an unpadded body) and `children`. It is a `Card` (so it keeps the theme's fill, border, radius and shadow) with no padding of its own; the parts are inside it:

- **Header strip**: always present. `SectionHeading` at the left (a node `heading` is rendered as given in an `h3`-type flex row instead, in the text colour: no mono, no accent), `aside` at the right (pushed to the right end; the two wrap under each other on a narrow screen).
- **Body**: padded 16 px with a 12 px gap between children, or `flush` with neither, for a list or a table whose rows bring their own padding. A flush list is a `ul` with `listSx` (a hairline between rows) holding rows with `listRowSx` (below).
- **Foot strip**: `foot` is laid out as a wrapping row with its children at the ends (`justify-content: space-between`): pass one child for a caveat, two for a count at the left and a link or paging at the right.
- Every `Panel` takes this look at once; there is no variant. The strips take the card's corner radius (`inherit`), so the Card does not need `overflow: hidden` (a map tooltip is never cut). A flush body with no foot clips its own bottom corners (`overflow: hidden`), so the last row's hover fill follows the card's radius; a padded body never clips.
- No colour, shadow or amber of its own: the strip fill and `line` are the existing ones.

**List rows in a panel.** On the pages other than the main screen: a `listSx` list (a hairline between rows) whose `li`s take `listRowSx` (`domain/utils/listSx.ts`: 12 px 16 px padding, `raised` hover fill). A full-width Record row (`RecordResultRow`) is 14 px 20 px. The main screen's rows are unchanged at 20 px (`ROW_INSET`, with `inset`).

## Tables (`theme` table overrides, `domain/molecules/PanelTable`, `domain/atoms/NumericCell`, #245)

The treatment is at two levels, so every table has it and no consumer opts in.

- **The theme** (`MuiTableCell`, `MuiTableRow`): a head cell takes the strip fill (`theme/stripFill.ts`, the one `FilterStrip` and `Panel` use; no new colour), mono 12 `muted`, and the `line` hairline below; every cell is 12 px 14 px (`padding="none"` and `"checkbox"` keep theirs); a row with `hover` fills with `raised` (a `selected` row keeps its `line` fill). The coverage grids (`CoverageGrid`, `CoverageGridCompact`, `CoverageCell`) are heat maps, not data tables: they set their own padding and a transparent head fill, and keep their look.
- **`PanelTable`** (molecule): the table's own sideways-scroll box around a `Table`, for a `Panel`'s `flush` body. Props: `label` (required: the scroll region's accessible name; the box is a focusable `region`), `minWidth` (px or a CSS length: below it the table scrolls inside the box instead of squeezing its columns; absent, it fits), and every `Table` prop, `sx` and `aria-label` included (`label` names the scroll box; the table's own name is `aria-label` or a `caption` child). The box is `overflow-x: auto`, `max-width: 100%`, `min-width: 0`, so at 390 px the table scrolls in its box and the page does not. Put `hover` on each body `TableRow` for the `raised` hover.
- **`NumericCell`** (atom): a `TableCell` for a number, in the head or the body: right-aligned, Plex Mono, tabular figures. It takes every `TableCell` prop, `sx` and ref; head or body size and colour come from the theme's cell (via the enclosing `TableHead`). A money amount inside it is still a `MoneyAmount`.
- Text columns stay `TableCell`s, left-aligned; a column that holds numbers has a `NumericCell` in its head and in every body row.

## The main screen's panels (#221: `domain/templates/MainScreenLayout`, `atoms/Well`, `molecules/InsetFigure`, `molecules/TotalFigure`, `molecules/FilterStrip`, `organisms/ResultTabs`)

Top to bottom: the title, the search panel, the Total panel, then the map panel and the results column (two columns from `md`, stacked below it; a column is `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so nothing makes the page scroll sideways). ONRC shares the template, so its title is in the same slot.

- **`MainScreenLayout` slots.** `title` (the `h1`, above every panel; the pages use `h2` styling, 24 px Space Grotesk), `search` (the top of the search panel, under the `Search` region name), `filters` (edge to edge under it, in the same panel, with no padding of its own: whatever fills it brings its own padding by wrapping itself in a `FilterStrip`), `summary` (the Total, in its own panel), `maps` (the shown lens fills the map panel's body), `mapBack` (the right end of the map panel's header strip; the lens toggle is on its left), `mapNote` (the foot, behind a hairline), `busy` (`BusyRegions`: `summary` and `map` show their last answer while the next loads, dimmed to `BUSY_OPACITY` 0.6 with `aria-busy="true"`; the map's header strip with the lens toggle and its foot stay at full strength; the results are not here, `ResultTabs` takes its own `busy`), `results` (the results column: it brings its own panel, so a Go-to card or a note sits above it). The search panel clips its strips to its corners (`overflow: hidden`); the other panels do not, so a map tooltip is never cut.
- **Search.** The Source eyebrow is the visible label above the input well; the input keeps its accessible name (`copy.label`, "Search") and the eyebrow stays its description. With more than one Source the `SourcePicker` sits inside the search field, at its end (`SearchBar`'s `endSlot`): small segments, a native select (capped at 120 px, ellipsis) below `md`. `SearchBar` holds the height of its helper line when it has no hint, so the minimum-length hint appearing does not move what is below.
- **Strips.** `FilterStrip` (molecule) is the shared strip: the padded, hairline-bounded band with the `rgba(13,16,20,.35)` fill. `FilterChips` and `OnrcFilterChips` use it for both strips. The filter strip and the active strip (`FilterChips`) are each `rgba(13,16,20,.35)` over the panel, between hairlines, edge to edge. The active strip is absent when no chip is on (`ActiveChips` renders nothing; there is no "No filters on." sentence). The county picked on the map is one of the chips ("County: Brașov", first in the row; `FilterChips`' `countyName` prop is the name the page shows, the code when absent), so the strip shows for a county alone; removing it unsets `county` and `uat` (`withoutCounty`, as the map's back link does) and "Clear all" does too. The filter bar's controls are the Signals switch and the filter menus (below).
- **`Well` (atom).** Ground fill, 1 px `line` (or `lineStrong` with `edge="lineStrong"`) border, card radius and `shadow.well`; layout and padding come from `sx`. Used by the map (inside `BaseMap`, so every map has it: the Money, zoomed county, Coverage and ONRC lenses, and the entity page's Flow lens), the inset figures and every segmented control's track (via `SegmentedTrack`). The search input takes the same fill and shadow in `SearchBar`'s `sx`.
- **`InsetFigure` (molecule).** A well with a label (mono 12, muted) over a value (`type.figure`: mono 18, 600), and an optional `note` (a node, mono 12 muted, a third line under the value: what the figure rests on). It grows from 200 px and wraps. With `href` the whole figure is the link, in the body colour; the note is part of the link.
- **`TotalFigure` has two layouts.** Stacked (default; the entity page, the Records tab and the Money lens use it and read as before). Inset (`inset={{ heading }}` plus copy with `figures`: `InsetTotalCopy`; only `MainTotal` opts in): the amount in a `2 1 360px` column (the container the amount's size is measured against, with the `heading` above it), then Records, suspect values (the link), no-value Records and framework ceilings, each an `InsetFigure` only when not zero. A zero Total shows the amount alone; a missing answer (the query failed) is `MainTotal`'s em dash, drawn by the app, not here. The labels are fixed text (`copy.figures`), so a count of one reads like many. Party coverage is a list, not one figure: `MainTotal` keeps it as a block under the figures.
- **Map panel.** `busy` (from the layout) dims only the body, not the header strip or the foot. Header strip (the lens toggle, a segmented control, on the left, `mapBack` on the right: `MapBackLink`, shown when a county is zoomed on the Money lens), the body (the lens's title, the map in its well, the legend, the tiles), the foot. The national Money map is titled `main.mapTitle` (`h2`); a zoomed county keeps `zoomedCounty.title`. `MainDataThrough` is a two-column definition list: the record kind as `dt`, "data through <date>" as `dd` (`DataThroughDate` takes `withoutKind`).
- **Results panel (`ResultTabs`).** The pinned Go-to card sits above the panel, outside it. The panel's header is the tab list (`Tabs`, no border of its own) with a `status` slot at its right end (the main screen puts a `LoadingStatus` there while any request is in flight), then the selected tab's rows (padded), then the foot. `busy` dims the rows and the pinned card to 60% with `aria-busy="true"` while the next answer loads; the header with the tabs and the status line stays at full strength, so the line is announced. Each tab's count is a pill (`TabLabel`: ground fill, `line` border, radius 10, mono 12, muted; the tab's accessible text is unchanged). "Show all n" is a centred outlined (raised) `Button` with an `href`, so it is still a link; it keeps the body colour.

## Filter menus (#224, #225: `atoms/Menu`, `atoms/MenuItem`, `molecules/FilterMenu`, `molecules/FilterBarRow`, `molecules/FilterBarDivider`, `molecules/ChoiceMenu`, `molecules/MultiChoiceMenu`, `YearMenu`)

- **`Menu` and `MenuItem` (atoms).** Pass-through wrappers of MUI's (portalled; focus moves into the list on open; arrows move; Escape calls `onClose` and MUI returns focus to the anchor). The theme gives the menu the overlay surface, 6 px padding with 2 px between items, and puts it 8 px below its anchor (`marginTop` on the paper). A `MuiMenuItem` override gives items `size.menuItem` (40), the control radius, a 10 px side padding, `text2` colour (`text` when `aria-checked` or `selected`) and the `hover` fill. A `MenuItem` must sit inside a `Menu` (MUI's list context); a `Menu`'s direct children cannot be fragments.
- **`FilterMenu` (molecule, shared by `FilterChips` and `OnrcFilterChips`).** The button is an outlined (raised) `Button`, 44 px, `radius.menuButton` (22, a full pill): the group name in muted, the value in `text2` (or `unsetLabel`, "Any" / "Oricare", when `value` is `undefined`), a chevron. Set: the value in `text` and the selected state's inset accent outline (without `aria-pressed`, which would make it a toggle). Open: the `line` fill, `aria-expanded="true"`, `aria-haspopup="menu"`; the menu is named by the button. The owner holds the filter; `FilterMenu` holds only open or closed. `children` is the menu's contents, or a function that gets `{ close }`: single choice calls `close` on a pick, a multi-select does not, and any other content (the year menu's two columns) goes in as it is. `listSx` styles the list the children sit in. The arrow keys step through the items in DOM order (MUI moves focus only across direct children), so a two-column list is a CSS grid that flows by column with the items in reading order, and the arrows carry on from one column into the next. `defaultOpen` starts it open, for stories. While the menu is open MUI marks the rest of the page `aria-hidden`, the button included (query it with `hidden: true`).
- **`MenuOption`.** One line: label on the left, amber check on the right when chosen; `role` is `menuitemradio` (single choice) or `menuitemcheckbox` (multi-select), with `aria-checked`. Optional `label` (accessible name when the text alone is ambiguous) and `hint` (tooltip). `MenuHeading` is a column heading inside a menu: not an item, never focused.
- **`ChoiceMenu` (molecule, shared by `FilterChips` and `OnrcFilterChips`).** `FilterMenu` over `MenuOption`s as `menuitemradio`; picking calls `onChoose(value)` and closes; the owner unsets when the value is the chosen one (`toggleChannel`, `toggleValueBucket`). Channel, Value and Party use it (Party's owner rule: the chosen list again clears it; it is single choice because the `party` filter holds one list). It replaces `ChoiceChips`, so these menus have no named group; their names are the buttons'. The copy key `FilterChipsCopy.menuAny` is the unset text.
- **`MultiChoiceMenu`.** `FilterMenu` over `MenuOption`s as `menuitemcheckbox`; stays open on a pick; the owner adds or removes (`toggleProcedure`, `toggleBadge`). The button shows `undefined` (Any) for none, the one pick's name, or `picked(n)` ("3 picked" / "3 alese"; `pickedText`). With no options it shows `emptyText` as its only, disabled line. `ProcedureMenu` and `BadgeMenu` map their data onto it (badge explanations are the items' tooltips; the group name of the Badges menu is `badges.menu`).
- **`YearMenu`.** Two columns, "From year" and "To year", each "Any" then the years, as `menuitemradio` items named `From year 2023` (one choice per column); it stays open. The button shows `yearRangeText`: `from 2023`, `until 2025`, `2023–2025`, or the bare year when both ends match (`din 2023`, `până în 2025` in Romanian).
- **`HasSignalsChip` (the Signals switch, #223).** First in the filter bar, followed by `FilterBarDivider` (1 px, 28 px `lineStrong` rule). A 44 px, radius-22 toggle button with `aria-pressed` (`true` only for `hasSignals=true`; `false` shows it off and the active row carries "Without Signals"): the amber `SignalMark`, the label in 600, and a 34 by 20 px `SwitchTrack`. Off: `raised` fill, `signalLine` border, `lineStrong` track, `text2` knob. On: `accentTint` fill, `accent` border, `accent` track, `bg` knob, no halo and no inset highlight (the raised shadow's drop only); it overrides the theme's shared selected treatment. The knob and track transitions (120 ms) are off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The copy's `signals` has `has` and `without` only.
- **`FilterBarRow` (molecule, shared by `FilterChips` and `OnrcFilterChips`).** The row the Signals switch and the menus sit in (on ONRC's screen, its menus and fields), 8 px apart. From `md` it wraps. Below `md` it is one row that scrolls sideways inside the strip (`overflow-x: auto`, no wrapping, Signals first), with a fade on the right edge that sits outside the scroller so it stays put; the page itself never scrolls sideways. Checked by style assertions and the `PhoneStrip` story; a human should eyeball it at 390 px.

## The base map (`domain/organisms/BaseMap`)

The shared map every lens builds on (Money, zoomed county, Coverage, Flow). It has no lens logic: a lens passes numbers per area and decides what they mean.

- `topology` (TopoJSON, built at load time, never GraphQL) with features carrying properties `{ code, name }`: `code` is an ISO 3166-2:RO county code or a SIRUTA; `name` is shown as published. Other shapes: pass `readArea`. `object` picks the TopoJSON object (first by default).
- `values`: `Record<code, number | null>`. Missing, null or non-finite = the `map.none` fill (`NO_DATA_COLOR`, always distinct from the scale). `scale`: `"linear"` (spans zero to the maximum, so colour tracks absolute size; `domain` fixes the span) or `"log"` (zeros are not log-scaled: they take the lowest colour and the legend's low end reads "up to ..."). `formatValue(value, code?)` formats tooltip, area names and legend ends; `code` is the area, so the Money lens looks up the exact Bani string by code and calls `formatMoney` on that, never on the number (on legend ends `code` is an area holding that value, if any).
- `onSelect(code)` on click, Enter or Space (areas are focusable buttons); without it areas are focusable images, not buttons. `selected` highlights one. `tiles` is a slot under the legend for off-map tiles. `renderTooltip(area)` overrides the tooltip content. `copy: BaseMapCopy` carries every string (`mapLabel`, `legendTitle`, `noData`, `areaLabel`, `atMost`). `width`/`height` set the SVG's coordinate space (it scales to its container); `projection` is `"conic"` (default) or `"mercator"`.
- Extension points for lenses (the map itself stays lens-free): `colorOf(code)` overrides the numeric fill (a categorical colour; null = grey), `describeArea({code,name,value})` replaces the value text in the accessible name and default tooltip (a category name; null = the formatted value), `legend` replaces the numeric legend (a categorical one that owns its no-data swatch), `renderOverlay({ centroids, project, width, height })` draws marks (a circle per UAT, a mark per Source) inside the SVG over the areas, pointer events off. Stories: `CategoricalFill`, `OverlayCircles`.
- Dark ground (#98): areas are separated by a stroke in `map.gap` (the panel colour); hover and selection use `grafStates.mapArea`; the tooltip is centred on the top edge of the hovered area (`anchors` from `projectFeatures`); layout is the map, then the legend, then the `tiles` slot. `NO_DATA_COLOR` is `map.none`, a fill close to the panel, so every "none" swatch carries a `NONE_OUTLINE` line. The numeric ramp runs `RAMP_FROM`..`RAMP_TO` of d3's Blues (the darkest blues fail 3:1 on the panel). `renderValue(value, code?)` draws the default tooltip and legend ends as nodes (the money lenses pass `moneyValueRenderer`, a `MoneyAmount`). Lens tooltips use the `meta` variant throughout (name `text` 600, values `text2`): `AreaFigures` and `CoverageFigures` take `dense` for it (tiles keep their own look). `ADJUSTED_BLUE` is the one blue for Coverage HARVESTED and Flow arcs. `BaseMap/mapSwatches.test.ts` asserts every map swatch reaches 3:1 against the panel (a new swatch goes in its list). Map stories use the `onPanel` decorator (`@grivei/ui/workbench`).
- Pure helpers (`createColorScale`, `featuresOf`) are exported for lenses that need the same colours elsewhere.
- Story and test outlines are invented (`domain/samples/geo/`, built by `packages/ui/scripts/build-geo-fixtures.ts`).

## The Money lens map (`domain/organisms/MoneyLensMap`)

`MoneyLensMap` is the county-level Money lens on the base map. Presentational: `map: MoneyMap`, `topology`, `selected?: MapArea`, `onSelect(area)`, `side` + `onSideChange(side)` (the URL's `countySide` is the only source of the side), `locale`, `copy: MoneyLensMapCopy` (`map`, `side`, `tiles`). A county click emits its code; a tile click emits `NATIONAL`, `UNPLACED` or `UNKNOWN_SUPPLIER_COUNTY`. Counties are coloured on a linear scale from the awarded bani (colour only; every shown amount is the exact Bani string through `formatMoney`). Reusable pieces: `MapTile` (a toggle-button tile), `SideToggle`, `OffMapTiles` (National always; Unplaced and unknown-supplier-county only when the map has them), `moneyMapValues`, `totalsByCounty`, `moneyValueFormatter` (the base map's `formatValue`: the exact Bani string by code, never the number; use it for every money lens).

## The zoomed county map (`domain/organisms/ZoomedCountyMap`)

`ZoomedCountyMap` is the Money lens zoomed into one county (authority side), on the base map. Presentational: `map: UatMap` (`seap.search.uatMap(county:)`), `topology` (the county's UAT outlines with properties `{ code: SIRUTA, name }`; Bucharest's are its six sectors, same component), `selected?: Siruta`, `onSelect(siruta)` (click, Enter or Space: the `RecordFilter.uat` value), `showAuthorityCircles?` (off by default), `locale`, `copy: ZoomedCountyMapCopy` (`map`, `figures`, `authorities`, `circleKey`, `unknownUat`). UATs are coloured by `total.awarded` on a log scale (colour only; every shown amount is the exact Bani string through `formatMoney`). A UAT of the topology that `map.uats` lacks is grey and its text is `copy.map.noData`, which must say no authority bought anything, not "no data". Circles (`AuthorityCircles`, via `renderOverlay`) are sized by `entities` and decorative (`aria-hidden`); the tooltip adds the authority count only when they are on. `map.unknownUat` (authorities with a known county but no UAT) shows as a non-interactive row (`UnknownUatRow`, in the `tiles` slot) when non-null: the contract has no filter value for it. Reuses the Money lens's `moneyMapValues`, `moneyValueFormatter`, `totalsByCounty` (a UAT row has a county row's shape) and `AreaFigures`.

## The Coverage lens map (`domain/organisms/CoverageLensMap`)

`CoverageLensMap` is the Coverage lens at county level, on the base map. Presentational: `lens: CoverageLens` (the `CoverageLens` query: `sources` and `coverage`), `topology`, `selected?: MapArea`, `onSelect(area)` (a county code, `NATIONAL` or `UNPLACED`), `locale`, `copy: CoverageLensMapCopy` (`map`, `status`, `noStatus`, `marks`, `tooltip`, `tiles`). Counties are filled by `status` through the base map's `colorOf` (a category, never a ratio: `values` stays empty, so nothing encodes N or M); a county without a row gets its own `NO_STATUS_COLOR` (the dark ground's `map.none` fill, with a `NONE_OUTLINE` swatch; apart from the not-harvested grey) and says `copy.noStatus`, which must not read as "not harvested" or "bought nothing". `NOT_HARVESTED` shares `PLANNED_MARK_COLOR` (grey = not harvested, as in the glossary), so the planned Sources' marks and a not-harvested county match. ## The search bar (`domain/molecules/SearchBar`)

`SearchBar` is the free-text search box. Presentational: it emits the query, the page fetches. Props: `value` (the committed query, the URL's `q`), `onQueryChange(query)`, `loading?` (a small spinner, `aria-busy` on the `role="search"` wrapper), `debounceMs?` (250), `minLength?` (2), `describedBy?` (the id of an element that describes the field, the Source eyebrow's `id`; added to the field's `aria-describedby` after the hint), `endSlot?` (a control inside the field at its end, after the clear button: the main screens put the inline `SourcePicker` there), `copy: SearchBarCopy` (`label`, `placeholder`, `hint?`, `clear`, `loading`, `minLengthHint`; the field is 56 px, `hint` is the line under it and gives way to `minLengthHint` while the query is one character; with no `hint` there is no line until the query is too short: ONRC's screen has none, its placeholder says it). In the app: pages pass `onQueryChange={(q) => setFilter({ q: q || undefined })}` (history is replaced, never pushed). The main screen passes `loading` only while a typed query is the change in flight (a request is out for a `q` other than the last answered one), so a filter click does not spin the field; the other pages pass their query's loading state. Aborting a superseded request is the page hook's job: `useFullResults` passes an `AbortController` signal to the client, aborts it when the variables change or the page unmounts, and ignores an aborted answer. The main screen's three hooks (`useMainScreen`, `useZoomedCounty`, `useCoverageLens`) share `useHeldQuery`: `loading` only with no answer, `ready` with `stale: true` while the next request is in flight, `error` with a `retry`. - **`SegmentedTrack`** (molecule) is the track: a `Well` with the group's own border off, as wide as its segments. It adds no element between the group and its buttons, so the roving focus (arrow keys, Home, End), the group's `aria-label` and each button's `aria-pressed` are MUI's, unchanged. `OptionToggle` and `SourcePicker` both use it; nothing else builds a track.
- **Selected state**: the shared `selected` fragment (`theme/states.ts`: `line` fill, `text` colour, inset 1 px accent outline), which the theme gives every `ToggleButton`'s `.Mui-selected`. It is the same state as the current page in `PageNav`. Hover and pressed are the shared `hover` and `pressed` fragments.
- **`OptionToggle`** props: `options` (display order), `value`, `onChange(value)`, `labels` (text per option), `label` (the group's accessible name). Exclusive: clicking the chosen option again emits nothing.
- **`SignalOrderToggle`** props: `value` (`DATE_DESC` or `VALUE_DESC`), `onChange`, `copy` (`label`, `options` per order); a thin `OptionToggle`.
- **`SourcePicker`** props: `options` (`id`, `label`, optional `count`), `selected`, `onSelect`, `inline?`, `copy` (`label`). On a panel it sits on the track. The `inline` form (inside the search field, which is already a well) keeps no second track, only small segments; below `md` it is the native select, as before.
- Stories: every option selected, in both languages; `SourcePicker` on a panel and inline.

## The Source picker, the ONRC screens and the per-Source switches (#178)

The URL names the Source with `source=onrc` (SEAP is the default and is never written, so every link from before the picker is unchanged; `urlState/codec.ts` `sourceOf`, `UrlState.source`, written last). The ONRC screens keep ONRC's own state in the URL through `urlState/onrcScreen.ts` (`decodeOnrcScreen`/`encodeOnrcScreen`, `useOnrcUrlState`): the `ONRC_FILTER_URL` keys, `page`, `lens`, then `source=onrc`. A SEAP chip is never read as an ONRC one and the other way round.

- **`SourcePicker`** (molecule): one Source of the explorable ones as a segmented control, each option with an optional count (already worded in the Source's own unit: Records for SEAP, registrations for ONRC; never summed or compared). It is the picker inside the search bar (`inline`, passed as `SearchBar`'s `endSlot`: small segments, and below the `md` breakpoint a native dropdown with the same options and counts, `components/SourceSelect`, `hooks/useNarrowScreen`), the Records tab's Source switch and the Coverage lens and page's Source selector (the last two without counts). App side: `sources/sourceCounts.ts` (`pickerOptions`), `sources/useSourceCounts.ts` (the Source the page shows gives its own count; the other one is asked for the query text alone, which is what a switch shows; no query, no counts), `sources/sourceLinks.ts`.
- **ONRC screen** (`pages/OnrcPage`, shown by `Routes` for the main screen and the full-results URL when `source=onrc`): `MainScreenLayout` with `OnrcFilterChips`, `OnrcCountMap` (the count lens on the base map plus an Unplaced tile), `OnrcResults` (match count, `OnrcResultRow`s in the API's order, `PageNav`) and, as the second lens, `SnapshotCoverage`. Results are ONRC's alone (ADR 0005). Rows link to the entity page as a supplier (an entity with no SEAP role opens there). The ONRC search bar has no hint line (its placeholder says it).
- **`OnrcFilterChips`** is laid out as `FilterChips` (the other-pages handoff, `docs/handoffs/grivei-explorer-other-pages`): a `FilterStrip` holding one `FilterBarRow` with "In SEAP" as a `ChoiceMenu` (Any, SEAP counterparties only, Not in SEAP; picking the chosen one again unsets it), a `FilterBarDivider`, `MultiChoiceMenu` for Status (`menuitemcheckbox` items, the menu stays open; none ticked is no status filter and reads "Any", tick to include: a registration carries several statuses and matches when it has any ticked; one ticked names it, several read `picked(n)`; `toggleStatus`), `CaenField` (a 44 px pill-shaped `Well`, "CAEN" inside in muted, the hint as placeholder; sent on blur or Enter, digits only) and `RegisteredMenu` (a `FilterMenu` with `popup="dialog"` whose content is the From and To date fields; the button shows the range; Escape from a date field still closes it). `copy.any` is the unset text of both menus. Then, only when something is set, a second strip with `OnrcActiveChips` (the county is the count lens's click and shows there as "County: X"). The Records tab's ONRC registrations use the same bar.
- **Records tab**: when both Sources have something for the entity, a `SourcePicker` above the Records offers SEAP Records and ONRC registrations (`pages/EntityPage/hooks/useEntityOnrc.ts`: ONRC's search by the CUI, rows with exactly that CUI kept). A Source with nothing is not offered and shows nothing. Each keeps its own filter in the URL (`?source=onrc&status=...`); a switch starts the other unfiltered.
- **Coverage**: `CoveragePageLayout`'s `sourceSelector` slot and `CoverageLensSection`'s `sourceSelector` hold the selector. ONRC has no quarter grid, so its Coverage is `SnapshotCoverage`: one row per snapshot (a cell's `quarter` is the snapshot's date, or a month for a gap), a known gap with no rule reads `missingSnapshot` ("No snapshot of this month is held.").
