why each gap is there

  1. DAN · 2025 Q2 · known gapThe file on data.gov.ro for this quarter is mis-uploaded and holds no DAN rows. Entered by hand, and reported to the publisher.
  2. Contracts · 2024 Q4 · possibly incomplete95,000 rows, against 124,000 to 229,000 in other quarters. Flagged by the published rule: fewer than 60% of the median row count of the surrounding quarters.
  3. Awards without a prior call · 2021 Q1 to 2022 Q4 · not in the sourceThe quarterly exports for these years leave out procedures with no prior call. A backfill from e-licitatie.ro will cover them. Until it has run, these contracts are missing from totals and Signals.
  4. Current quarter · 2026 Q4Contracts and award notices are crawled every night and stay provisional until the quarterly export lands. Direct purchases are not crawled, so they wait for the bulk file.

per county: N of M

On the map, each county reads “N of M public entities have SEAP records since 2021, plus K other contracting authorities”. M comes from the Ministry of Finance list of public entities, snapshot of [DATE]. An entity without records may simply have bought nothing, so the count is never shown as a percentage.
